# CampusPilot — Canonical UX Copy & Microcopy Guide

**Phase:** Phase 1 Baseline  
**Author:** Angel (Team Lead & UX Lead)  
**Target Audience:** Frontend Team (Nidhi) & Reviewers  

---

## 1. Brand Tone & Voice Principles
- **Modern & Energetic:** Speaks like a smart, proactive campus companion.
- **Student-First:** Direct, concise, relatable; never sounds like a stiff ERP circular.
- **Transparent & Action-Oriented:** Every card answers *"What is it, when is it, and what should I do?"*
- **No Fluffy AI Jargon:** Never say "Our neural algorithm thinks..."; say "Matches your Technical interest."

## 3. Home Screen Copy

### Personalized Header Greetings
- **Morning (05:00–11:59):** `Good morning, [First Name] 👋`
- **Afternoon (12:00–16:59):** `Good afternoon, [First Name] ☀️`
- **Evening (17:00–04:59):** `Good evening, [First Name] 🌙`
- **Sub-greeting:** `Here's what matters on your campus today.`

### Section Headers & Subtitles

#### 1. 🔥 Section 1: Don't Miss
- **Header:** `🔥 Don't Miss`
- **Sub-tag:** `Urgent registrations & happening soon`
- **Urgency Pill Examples:**
  - `Closes Tonight`
  - `Starts in 2 Hours`
  - `Limited Seats Left`

#### 2. ✨ Section 2: For You
- **Header:** `✨ For You`
- **Sub-tag:** `Personalized to your interests`
- **Match Explanation Tooltips:**
  - `94% match · Matches your Technical interest, happens today, registration closes tonight.`
  - `88% match · Matches your Cultural & Music interest.`
  - `82% match · Matches your Career & Placement goals.`

#### 3. 📢 Section 3: Important Campus
- **Header:** `📢 Important Campus`
- **Sub-tag:** `Official announcements from university offices`
- **Priority Badges:**
  - `CRITICAL NOTICE` (Red)
  - `OFFICIAL CIRCULAR` (Amber/Slate)

#### 4. 🧭 Section 4: Explore Something New
- **Header:** `🧭 Explore Something New`
- **Sub-tag:** `Curated beyond your selected interests`

## 5. My Plan Screen Copy

### Header & Summary
- **Header:** `📅 My Plan`
- **Subtitle:** `Your saved campus schedule`
- **Conflict Warning Banner:**
  - `⚠️ Time Conflict Detected`
  - *"[Event A] and [Event B] both take place on [Date] at [Time]. Choose which to attend."*
  - Conflict Action CTAs: `[Keep Event A]` | `[Keep Event B]`

## 7. Empty & Edge State Copy

### Empty Personalized Feed (No matches found)
> **Heading:** Nothing matched your selected interests for this week yet.  
> **Body:** Explore all campus activities or add more interests in your Profile.  
> **CTA Button:** `Browse All Campus Events`

### Empty "My Plan"
> **Heading:** Your campus plan is empty.  
> **Body:** Bookmark workshops, games, and fests to build your personal timeline.  
> **CTA Button:** `Find Something to Attend`

### Empty "Alerts / Notifications"
> **Heading:** You're all caught up! ✨  
> **Body:** No urgent deadlines or critical notices right now.

### Zero Interests Selected (Skipped Onboarding)
> **Banner Text:** *Showing top campus highlights. Select your interests in Profile to personalize your feed!*
