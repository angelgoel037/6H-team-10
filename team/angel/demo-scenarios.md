# CampusPilot — Live Demo Scenarios & Judge Presentation Guide

**Phase:** Phase 1 Baseline  
**Lead Presenter:** Angel (Team Lead)  
**Demo Duration:** 2–3 minutes total  

---

## 🎬 Demo Overview & Pitch Hook (30 Seconds)

> *"Judges, college students miss out on hundreds of incredible hackathons, band nights, and sports trials every semester simply because campus life is scattered across 50 WhatsApp groups, posters, and Instagram stories.*
> 
> *Meanwhile, critical notices like MST schedules get buried under spam.*
> 
> *CampusPilot fixes this. **One campus. Thousands of experiences. One personalized feed.**"*

---

## 🚀 Scenario 1: Personalized Discovery & Urgency (Angel Persona)
**Persona:** Angel (3rd Year CSE — Interests: *Technical, Cultural, Sports, Career*)

### Live Click Path & Flow
1. **Open App:** Landing on Onboarding, select **Technical**, **Cultural**, and **Sports**. Click **"Build My Campus Feed"**.
2. **Personalized Home:**
   - Notice **"🔥 Don't Miss"**: Displays *"Campus AI & Robotics Hackathon 2026"* with badge `Registration Closes in 4 Hours`.
   - Notice **"✨ For You"**:
     - Card 1: *Campus AI & Robotics Hackathon* (`94% match` badge).
     - Card 2: *Acoustic Night & Open Mic* (`88% match` badge).
     - Card 3: *Basketball Championship Semifinals* (`82% match` badge).
   - Click the **"Why 94%?"** match explanation: Tooltip expands to show: *"Matches your Technical interest, happens this week, registration closes tonight."*
3. **Action:** Click `+ Add to Plan` on the AI Hackathon and Acoustic Night.

### Talking Point
> *"Look at how the feed adapts instantly. Angel doesn't see a random dump—he sees the hackathon closing tonight, the open mic happening tomorrow, and the basketball semifinal, each with explainable match scores."*

---

## 📢 Scenario 2: Institutional Integrity (Important Campus Override)
**Objective:** Show that critical academic and administrative notices NEVER get lost or filtered out.

### Live Click Path & Flow
1. Scroll down to **"📢 Important Campus"** section on the Home Screen.
2. Observe the prominent red badge: `CRITICAL NOTICE` on:
   - *"Mid-Semester Examination (MST) Official Datesheet Released"* (Office of the Controller of Examinations).
   - *"Even Semester Re-evaluation & Grade Improvement Results"*.
3. Click on the MST notification card to view the official details modal:
   - Displays exam dates, seating plan instructions, and administrative verification stamp.

### Talking Point
> *"Here is our core product promise: CampusPilot personalizes discovery, but it NEVER hides institutional reality. Whether a student selects coding, dance, or skips onboarding entirely, critical notices from the Examination Cell and Dean always remain front and center."*

---

## 🧭 Scenario 3: Intentional Serendipity & Time Conflict Resolution
**Objective:** Show Explore discovery, adding an event outside preferences, and smart calendar conflict detection.

### Live Click Path & Flow
1. **Explore Tab:** Click **Explore** in bottom navigation.
2. Filter by `🧠 Wellness`. Select *"Mindfulness & Stress Management for Midterms"*.
3. Click `+ Add to Plan`.
4. **My Plan Tab:** Navigate to **My Plan** in bottom navigation.
5. Notice the visual **`⚠️ Time Conflict Detected`** banner on the timeline:
   - *"AI Hackathon Prep and Mindfulness Session both overlap on October 19 at 4:00 PM."*
6. Click `[Keep AI Hackathon]` or `[Resolve Conflict]`.

### Talking Point
> *"CampusPilot doesn't trap you in a filter bubble. The Explore tab lets you discover wellness and arts sessions, while My Plan automatically flags schedule overlaps before you commit."*

## 🏆 Final Wrap-up (15 Seconds)
> *"In just 2 minutes, we saw personalized discovery, guaranteed institutional communication, serendipitous exploration, and actionable time planning. CampusPilot turns campus chaos into a personalized companion for every student. Thank you!"*
